# Native role UI — October 24, 2026

Implemented approved [Figma flow](https://www.figma.com/design/L4c5A5bH9pjnDCTFPDcwHs?node-id=3-27): Blind / Not blind each launch, large Call button, native assistance startup, aspect-fit camera, translucent material islands, stable Stop/End, accessible details and separate tools. Helper supports authenticated watch-only sessions/invitations/join/leave. Missing service configuration and telemetry are explicit. Normal Call does not yet publish friend video, and full friend audio, shared hand/object/distance telemetry and elevator-button guidance remain future integration.
