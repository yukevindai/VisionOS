01 / Our mission

Everyday moments.
More room to participate.

Finding your cup. Following a shared moment. Choosing what comes next.

Our mission is to make useful context more accessible through sound and wearable feedback, while keeping the person in control. We’re starting with small, everyday tasks and building from there.

02 / What we’re building

Support for the little things
that open up bigger things.

01

Find what you’re
looking for.

Ask for an everyday object. The prototype identifies and tracks a target, then uses available hand and depth information to guide a reach.

Object finding + hand guidance

Prototype capability. Performance depends on camera view, depth quality and the test setup.

02

Understand the scene.

Ask what’s around you and hear a description of the visible scene. Audio makes the result available without needing to look at a screen.

03

Stay in the moment.

We’re exploring ways to explain recent visible events, so shared experiences can come with more context. This direction is still being developed.

03 / The technology

A camera sees. AI interprets.
Guidance reaches you.

The wearable is the product we’re building. The camera and laptop support today’s prototype.

  1. 01 / Capture

    A view of the scene

    An iPhone supplies the camera feed and available depth information.

  2. 02 / Understand

    Context, on request

    The laptop processes vision and voice requests, connecting scene analysis with object tracking.

  3. 03 / Respond

    Sound + wearable feedback

    Audio explains the result. The hardware work brings physical feedback into the loop.

04 / Our story

Built with curiosity.
Driven by purpose.

We started VisionOS at Cal Hacks with a question: how can AI move beyond a screen and offer useful help in the physical world?

We’re a team building an early assistive-hardware prototype. Our next step is to learn from blind and low-vision people, accessibility practitioners, and hardware collaborators, so the problems we work on reflect real priorities.
