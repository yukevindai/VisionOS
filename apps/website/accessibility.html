Accessibility

We want the VisionOS company website to be useful to everyone, including blind and low-vision visitors.

Using this website

  • Navigate with your keyboard. A skip link appears when focused, and links have visible focus indicators.
  • Hardware photographs include text descriptions. Headings and landmarks organize the page for screen readers.
  • Text and layouts adapt to smaller screens and browser zoom.
  • The site follows your light or dark appearance preference, respects reduced motion, and does not autoplay video or audio.
  • Technology details expand using native disclosure controls. Press Enter or Space on a focused heading to open or close them.

Tell us about a barrier

If something prevents you from using this site, please report an accessibility issue on GitHub. Include the page, what you were trying to do, and your browser or assistive technology if you’re comfortable sharing it. GitHub requires an account and issues are public; please avoid sharing private information.

Our current stage

This website introduces an early hardware prototype. Website accessibility is separate from the usability and safety validation of the hardware. Automated checks do not replace testing with assistive technology and people who use it. We have not completed a formal accessibility audit or live-device acceptance testing for this website release.
